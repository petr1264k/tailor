TAILOR (working name)
The web, rewritten for the one person reading it.
The Living Internet Build Day · AGI House × Coframe · Sat Oct 10 2026
Team: Petr (frontend), Mikhail (ex-unicorn CTO), + one LLM-agent engineer recruited at 12:00

========================================================================
ONE-LINER
========================================================================
Your AI chats already know you. Tailor turns your ChatGPT and Claude history into a private preference profile, and a Chrome extension rewrites any web page for you. It learns from how you use each page, and it never invents a single fact.


========================================================================
THE PROBLEM
========================================================================
- Websites personalize for segments ("electronics shopper, 25-44"), built from cookies and guesses. None of them knows you.
- Your AI assistants do. Over months you have told ChatGPT and Claude your gear, your budget, your diet, your job, your stack, and how you like things explained. That knowledge is locked inside each company's app, and no website can use it.
- So every page you visit is written for the average visitor. You still dig through marketing copy, specs that don't matter to you, and examples in the wrong language or at the wrong level.


========================================================================
THE INSIGHT
========================================================================
Your AI chat history is the most accurate description of you that exists: thousands of messages of real intent, in your own words. Browsing history shows what you clicked. Your chats show what you wanted and why. And you can already export it through the official export features.


========================================================================
HOW IT WORKS
========================================================================
1. IMPORT
   You export your data from ChatGPT (Settings > Data controls > Export data) and/or Claude (Settings > Privacy > Export data). You drop conversations.json into the extension. We take that one file, not the whole zip, because exports can be gigabytes once images and voice notes are included. The extension keeps only your own messages, since that's where preferences live, and stores them locally in the browser.

2. EXTRACT
   A cheap, fast model (Claude Haiku 5.5) reads your messages in chunks and proposes candidate preferences, each with a short supporting quote. A second pass merges duplicates and scores each fact by how often and how recently it appears. A large archive costs about a dollar to process.

3. FILTER
   Sensitive topics are never extracted: health, religion, politics, sexuality, exact location, detailed finances, and other people's names. Code and a classifier both enforce this, and the UI shows a "never extracted" list with counts.

4. PROFILE
   You see your profile as a list of facts. Each one shows where it came from ("ChatGPT, Aug 3: 'just got a PS5, which TV should I get?'"). You can keep, edit or delete any fact. The profile is grouped by source, so you can see what ChatGPT knows about you, what Claude knows, and where they overlap.
   Example facts: owns a PS5 · big purchases around $1,000 · prefers short answers and bullet points · writes TypeScript · vegetarian · chats in Russian · asks for simple explanations about finance.

5. TAILOR
   On any page, the extension extracts the main content (Mozilla Readability, plus structured product/recipe/article data the page embeds). It sends that content and ONLY the profile facts relevant to this page to a fast model. The model returns a page built from a fixed set of components, not free-form HTML, so nothing breaks:
     - summary: one line, written for you
     - "for you" notes: tie a page fact to your situation ("Works with your PS5: 2 HDMI 2.1 ports, 120 Hz")
     - key facts table: the specs that matter to you, first
     - compare table, steps, warnings (e.g. an allergen if you mentioned one)
     - reordered original sections
     - collapsed fluff, each with a reason ("marketing copy collapsed: you prefer short answers")
     - links: only links that exist on the original page
   It's written in the language you chat in, at the reading level you ask for.
   Rewriting starts as soon as the page loads, so clicking Tailor feels instant. Results are cached per page and profile version.

6. FIDELITY GATE (code only, no model)
   Before anything is shown, code pulls every number, price, spec, model name and quoted claim out of the rewritten page and checks that each one appears in the original page. Anything that doesn't is stripped and counted. Facts that come from your own profile (like your budget) are labeled as yours. Rule of thumb: the model proposes, code decides.

7. RENDER IN PLACE
   The page rewrites itself in place, isolated so the site's styles don't clash. A floating switch flips between Original and Tailored. Every change has a "why" chip that opens the exact quote from your chats.

8. LEARN (the loop)
   Every tailored page watches how you use it:
     - you expand a collapsed section
     - you flip back to the original
     - you open a "why" chip
     - you ignore a "for you" note
     - you spend time on a block
   These signals update your profile. New behavior-based facts appear next to the chat-based ones, with their own evidence ("learned on shop.example, Oct 10: expanded specs twice"). The next page comes out differently. The interface keeps optimizing itself for the one person using it.


========================================================================
WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
========================================================================
- vs. reader mode and AI summarizers: they don't know you. Tailor reorders, filters and explains for your situation, cites why, and needs no setup.
- vs. AI assistants and browsers that remember you: they keep your profile locked inside one company and answer in a chat sidebar. Tailor merges your ChatGPT AND Claude history into a profile you own, rewrites the page itself, and checks every fact against the original.
- vs. earlier "reshape the web" extensions (built at AGI House before; DonnaOS didn't make the finals, EnableOS won only on its accessibility angle): Tailor needs no setup, shows evidence for every change, has a code-checked fidelity guarantee, learns from use, and comes with measured results.
- vs. site-side personalization (Coframe, Mutiny): sites optimize for segments. Tailor is the last mile for one person, on every site.


========================================================================
TRUST AND PRIVACY
========================================================================
- Local-first: the archive and profile live in your browser. There's no server and no account.
- Minimum sharing: each page gets only the few facts relevant to it.
- Sensitive topics are never extracted, and you see the list of what was skipped.
- Every fact is editable and deletable, with a source quote.
- The fidelity gate guarantees no invented numbers, prices or specs.
- On stage we show only owner-approved facts and short quotes, never raw chats.


========================================================================
STRETCH FEATURES (only if the core is done by 17:00)
========================================================================
- Agent handoff: the extension exposes a tool like get_preferences_for(page), so your own AI agent can use your profile (with your consent) while it browses or shops for you. This covers the event's second theme, agents as first-class users. (The browser's WebMCP API name is in flux; check Chrome's docs first, or use an MCP server.)
- Share with sites: one tap shares three consented facts with a site that asks for them. Coframe's own site already has a share_visitor_needs tool that asks agents for exactly this.
- Living profile: a content script on chatgpt.com and claude.ai adds new chats to your archive as you have them, so the profile updates without re-exporting.
- Tailor a judge-suggested URL live.
- Gemini import (Google Takeout).

Not building: accounts, syncing, mobile, payments.


========================================================================
THE NUMBERS WE'LL SHOW (all measured on the day, N stated out loud)
========================================================================
1. Questions answered on the first screen (the main number).
   For each person and page, generate 5 questions that person would actually ask, based on their profile ("Does this TV work with my PS5?"). The answer must be text copied exactly from the page; code verifies this. Then code checks whether each answer appears on the first screen of the original page versus the tailored page.
   Example result: "1 of 5 on the original, 5 of 5 tailored." 4 pages × 2 people × 5 questions = 40 checks.
2. Profile precision: each owner marks every extracted fact right or wrong (5 minutes per person).
3. Blind test: for 5 pages, show each person a version tailored to them and one tailored to a teammate, unlabeled. They pick theirs X of Y times (guessing gets 50%).
4. Invented facts shown to users: 0. Plus how many unsupported claims the gate caught and stripped.
5. Speed: time to first tailored content, and words before vs. after.
6. Learning: how the profile changed after N interactions, and how the next page differed.

BUILD PLAN
========================================================================
Stack
- Chrome extension (Manifest V3), TypeScript, Vite with @crxjs/vite-plugin, React for the import screen and profile viewer, and plain DOM in a shadow root for the in-page render.
- IndexedDB for the archive (request the unlimitedStorage permission).
- Libraries: jszip (only if we accept zips), @mozilla/readability.
- Models: Haiku 5.5 for extraction, Sonnet 5.5 for merging, and Haiku 5.5 or Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite for rewriting (whichever is faster on the day).
- API calls from the extension's background script. For browser-side calls to Anthropic's API, set the header anthropic-dangerous-direct-browser-access: true. A tiny local proxy is the fallback.

Data formats (freeze at 12:15)
  Fact = {
    id, category: reading | visual | language | expertise | interest | constraint | owns | dislikes | goal,
    value: "prefers bullet points over prose",
    confidence: 0..1,
    source: "chat" | "behavior",
    evidence: [{ origin: chatgpt | claude | page-url, title, date, quote }],
    userStatus?: kept | deleted | edited
  }
  Profile = { version, facts: Fact[], stats: { conversations, messages, from, to } }
  Block = summary | for_you(text, factIds) | key_facts(rows) | compare(columns, rows) | steps(items)
        | warning(text, factIds) | section(heading, text) | hidden(label, reason) | cta(label, href on the original page)
  TailoredPage = { url, profileVersion, blocks: Block[], gate: { checked, stripped[] } }
  Interaction = { url, profileVersion, kind: expand | collapse | toggle_original | open_why | ignore | dwell, blockType, factIds?, ms?, at }


========================================================================
RISKS AND HOW WE HANDLE THEM
========================================================================
- Exports arrive late (they're emailed and can take hours) → request them tonight, both of us.
- Huge archives → import conversations.json only, your own messages only, the last 6-12 months, a token cap; run extraction in the afternoon.
- Messy real pages → Readability + embedded structured data; 4 tested demo pages with offline copies; "any URL" only as a stretch.
- Slow rewrites → fast model, compact output, streaming, pre-tailor on page load, cache.
- Personal chats on a projector → owner-approved facts and short quotes only; each owner reviews their profile before 19:30.
- "It's just another reshape-the-web extension" → lead with the AI-history profile, the evidence links, the learning loop, the fidelity gate and the numbers.
- "Doesn't ChatGPT memory already do this?" → the answer in "What makes it different" above.
- Rules → everything is built at the event; nothing comes from any pre-existing project.


========================================================================
Q&A CHEAT SHEET
========================================================================
Privacy? Local-first, minimum sharing per page, sensitive topics never extracted, delete anything in one click, no server.
Just a summarizer? A summarizer doesn't know you. Tailor uses your context, cites why, needs no setup, and learns from use. The blind test shows people recognize their own page.
Hallucinations? The fidelity gate checks every number, price and spec against the original page and strips the rest. We showed the count.
Doesn't ChatGPT memory do this? It stays inside one company and answers in a sidebar. Tailor merges ChatGPT and Claude into a profile you own and rewrites the page itself, with a fact check.
How does this help site owners / Coframe? Sites optimize for segments; this is the last mile for one person. Next: consented sharing of three facts with any site that asks, like Coframe's own share_visitor_needs.
Why chats, not browsing history? Browsing shows what you clicked. Chats show what you wanted and why, in your own words, and they keep updating.
How do you know the profile is right? Owners rated every fact (precision [P]%), plus the blind test and the first-screen check.
What if it's slow or wrong? The original is one click away; rewrites stream and are cached; unsupported claims are stripped.
Is using the exports allowed? It's your own data, exported through the official export feature.
Built today? Yes. The night before we only planned, made mockups and requested our data exports.
Business? A consumer extension distributed like Glasp, plus an API that lets sites accept consented profiles.
